SlideGenie is developed by Haris.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your SlideGenie account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to harismughl11122@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: "Delete My SlideGenie Account"
  3. Include the email address associated with your account

Alternatively, you can delete your account directly inside the app:

  1. Open SlideGenie app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion

Data that will be deleted

  • Your email address and account credentials
  • Your profile information
  • All presentations and projects you created
  • AI generation history
  • Subscription data (managed by Google Play / RevenueCat)

Data retention

All account data is deleted within 30 days of your request. Some data (like billing records) may be retained as required by law for up to 90 days.
