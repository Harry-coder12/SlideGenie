Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

SlideGenie is an AI-powered presentation application that allows users to create, edit, store, and export presentations. This Privacy Policy explains what information SlideGenie collects, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SlideGenie, we may process the following information:

  • Account email address.
  • Account name or full name.
  • Presentations and slides created by you.
  • Text and other content entered into presentations.
  • Images uploaded to the application.
  • AI prompts and generated presentation content.
  • Usage information required to manage AI generation limits.
  • AppSumo entitlement information when a license is redeemed.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and operate SlideGenie, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Saving presentations and slides.
  • Generating presentation content using AI services.
  • Processing uploaded images.
  • Managing usage limits and entitlements.
  • Supporting AppSumo lifetime-license activation.
  • Providing account deletion and related services.
  • Maintaining application security and reliability.

3. Supabase

SlideGenie uses Supabase for authentication, database services, storage, and server-side functions.

Account information, presentations, slide data, usage information, and uploaded files may be processed through Supabase services as necessary to provide the application.

4. AI Processing

SlideGenie uses Google Gemini through server-side application functions to generate presentation content.

Information necessary to generate your requested presentation may be sent to the AI service for processing. AI-generated output is returned to SlideGenie and associated with your presentation.

5. Uploaded Images and Storage

Images uploaded by users may be stored using SlideGenie's Supabase storage infrastructure.

Users should avoid uploading information that they do not have permission to share or that is unnecessarily sensitive.

6. AppSumo

If you activate SlideGenie using an AppSumo license, we may process information required to validate and maintain the associated entitlement.

AppSumo license information is used only as necessary to provide the applicable SlideGenie entitlement.

7. GitHub Pages

SlideGenie's public website and Privacy Policy are hosted using GitHub Pages.

GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to the hosted website according to its own privacy practices.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and presentation information for as long as necessary to provide the service, unless deletion is requested or the information is otherwise no longer required.

9. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SlideGenie account and associated application data through the available account deletion functionality.

Deletion may include the user's profile, presentations, slides, usage information, stored images, and authentication account, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal requirements.

10. Security

SlideGenie uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information processed through the application. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

SlideGenie is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SlideGenie's functionality, services, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the SlideGenie project through its GitHub repository.
